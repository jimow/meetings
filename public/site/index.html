Official County Platform

Secure, geofenced meeting attendance for Mandera County

Department heads and meeting owners create a meeting, share a QR code or short link, and capture verified attendance — only from people physically present at the venue.

Staff Login Learn more

Built for accountable public meetings

Accurate records of who attended county meetings, barazas, trainings and forums.

📍

Strict geofencing

Attendees can only sign in within a set radius of the venue. The location check runs on the server, not the phone.

📱

QR & short links

Share a scannable QR code or a short link. No app install — attendees sign in from any phone browser.

🛡️

Layered security

Passcodes, duplicate prevention, rate limiting and an audit trail keep records trustworthy.

✉️

SMS & email alerts

Attendees get instant SMS (Africa's Talking) and email (Resend) confirmation; owners get notified of each sign-in.

📄

Official exports

Download branded PDF and Word attendance sheets with the county header, venue and date — or CSV for analysis.

⚙️

Configurable forms

Each meeting owner chooses exactly which details to collect: name, ID, department, designation and more.

How it works

From setup to signed attendance in four steps.

Create

A meeting owner sets the title, venue, fields to capture and the geofence radius.

Share

Display the QR code or send the short link to attendees.

Sign in

Attendees fill the form on their phone; location and rules are verified instantly.

Export

Download the official attendance sheet as PDF, Word or CSV.

For county staff

Authorized officers can manage meetings and attendance records.
